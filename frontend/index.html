A closer look at the ink.

Skribl uses convolutional neural networks trained on MNIST digits and case-sensitive EMNIST letters. Each installation keeps its own learning collection. Confirmed examples are used in batches; an updated model must pass validation before replacing the active model.

Benchmark results describe the original model on dataset images. Photos and personal handwriting can perform differently. Similar forms such as C/c or I/l can be ambiguous. Full words in cursive, mixed letters and digits, and multi-line text are future extensions.
